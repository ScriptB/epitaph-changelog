Epitaph · Patch Notes

What's changed recently

A plain-language look at the last few updates. For the full, warts-and-all development log — every bug, every fix, every dead end — see HISTORY.md in this repository.

Combat & Survival

Healing now speeds up when you're well-fed

Previously, healing always ticked over at the same slow rate no matter how full your hunger bar was. Now, staying completely fed — full hunger and saturation — kicks you into a genuinely faster regeneration rate, the same way it works in the Java Edition of Minecraft. Let your hunger dip and healing slows back down to the old rate.

Eating well and staying topped up during a fight or a build session now actually pays off, instead of hunger only mattering once it's nearly empty.

Teleportation

Accepting a teleport request gives you a moment to settle in

Accept a friend's /tpa request and you'll no longer risk getting immediately shoved back off their plot if you don't already have permission to be there. For about two minutes after arriving, you can freely walk around and get your bearings.

Nothing else changes — you still can't build, break, use their generators, or touch anything you weren't already allowed to. It just stops the awkward "teleported in, instantly kicked out" moment.

Plot Protection

Plot borders now feel like a real wall

Walking onto a plot you don't have permission for used to sometimes teleport you — occasionally a long way, if your own plot was far off. Now it works like the world border itself: you're physically stopped and pushed back right at the edge, exactly where you tried to cross. No more surprise long-distance teleports for wandering somewhere you shouldn't.

If something ever does go wrong and someone gets stuck against terrain, there's still a safety net that gently sends them home after a few seconds — but that's now the rare backup plan, not the everyday behavior.

New Item

Trial Locator — find a Trial Chamber nobody has ever set foot in

A new item, the Trial Locator, can point you toward the coordinates of a genuine, untouched Trial Chamber — one that hasn't been generated or explored by anyone yet. It's purchasable for a decently large sum, and it's one-time use.

It can also occasionally be found by defeating a Breeze — always as a real item dropped on the ground, never handed straight to you, so you'll need to go pick it up like any other loot.

Every chamber it points to is a genuinely fresh one — the search is specifically designed so the same location is essentially never handed out twice.
