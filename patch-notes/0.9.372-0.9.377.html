Epitaph · Patch Notes

Update 0.9.372–377

A plain-language look at this stretch of updates. For the full, warts-and-all development log, see HISTORY.md in this repository.

Snapshot: August 7, 2026 (confirmed)
Moderation

Being banned from a plot actually removes you from it now

Repeatedly breaking the rules on someone else's plot is supposed to get you knocked out and locked out of that plot for a while. It never actually worked — the push that's supposed to physically remove you was silently failing every single time, so a "banned" player just stood there like nothing happened. Fixed. The knockback now genuinely fires, so crossing the threshold actually gets you removed.

So repeat troublemakers actually get put outside, not just told to stop.

Plot Protection

Villagers on your plot are properly protected again

There was a gap where a newer type of villager wasn't recognized by the protection system at all, so it could be hit and killed by anyone regardless of your plot's permissions. Fixed — every villager and wandering trader on your plot is now covered by the same permission check as everything else.

Your villagers shouldn't be an exception to your own plot's rules.

Spawners

Spawner boxes protect instantly now, and read walls more accurately

Building or adjusting a containment wall around a spawner used to take up to 20 seconds to actually stop spawns outside it, even though it looked fully sealed the moment you finished. That gap is closed — a wall protects the instant it's there. Wall detection itself also got more accurate, so oddly-shaped blocks like stairs are read correctly instead of occasionally being treated as open space.

A grinder should be safe to stand in the second you place the last block, not twenty seconds later.

Plots

Plot name tags sit centered above your plot again

The floating name (or team name) above a plot had drifted toward wherever the owner was standing and bobbed up and down depending on nearby terrain — reverted back to a fixed spot centered above the plot. It only moves now if something is actually built in the way, climbing just high enough to clear it.

One predictable place to look for it, every time.

Plot Protection

Lava and water buckets can't be used to grief your plot anymore

A lava or water bucket used to completely bypass plot permissions — anyone could empty one onto your build regardless of whether they had access. That's fixed: placing lava or water now requires the same build permission as placing an ordinary block. Nothing already in the world was touched — this only stops new griefing, it's not a lava-removal pass.

You shouldn't need to babysit your own plot to stop someone burning it down.
