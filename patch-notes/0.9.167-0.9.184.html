Epitaph · Patch Notes

Update 0.9.167–184

A plain-language look at this stretch of updates. For the full development log, see HISTORY.md.

PvP

🚫 Combat-logging got a lot harder

Once you're tagged in a fight, ender pearls no longer let you teleport away, and running into the world border no longer gives you a free rescue teleport out of danger. Disconnecting mid-fight now leaves a permanent mark against your name, and repeated offenders get a running warning on every future login.

Player Shops

🪧 Shop signs finally, reliably work

For a long stretch, signs on Player Shops either never appeared, showed the wrong details, or silently failed to update — this took several real attempts to fully track down. It's fixed now: placing a sign next to (or on top of) your shop chest reliably fills it in with the real price, stock, and seller name, and refreshes correctly from then on.

If a shop from before this fix still looks broken, breaking and replacing its sign (or running /shop setup again while looking at the chest) will fix it.

World & Dimensions

🌍 Plot protection now works in the Nether and the End

Building, breaking, and generators were only ever properly protected in the Overworld — coordinates in the Nether or End that happened to overlap an Overworld plot's numbers could be affected by permissions that had nothing to do with where you actually were. That's fixed, and generator eggs can no longer take root outside the Overworld at all.

Bug Fixes

🐛 Admin commands stopped silently failing offline

/plot:claim and other admin commands used to fail for a player who was genuinely online, just because of how the game resolves player names internally. They now match names directly and work reliably regardless.
