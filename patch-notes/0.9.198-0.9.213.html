Epitaph · Patch Notes

Update 0.9.198–213

A plain-language look at this stretch of updates. For the full development log, see HISTORY.md.

Snapshot: ~July 26, 2026
Combat

Java-style combat, with real attack timing

Combat now works like Java Edition instead of Bedrock's default: every weapon has its own real cooldown, and a swing does more damage the longer you've waited since your last hit. Spam-clicking barely scratches someone; a slow, deliberate, fully-charged swing hits hard, can crit while falling, and sweeps nearby enemies with a real sword.

A cooldown bar shows under your crosshair so you can see exactly how charged your next swing is, and it swaps to a different look when you're aiming at a target.

This took a genuinely long string of attempts to get the on-screen cooldown bar actually rendering right — if it ever looks off, that's worth reporting.

Plots

Faster, more accurate plot claiming

Claiming a new plot used to sometimes take far too long, or land you somewhere with a mountain running straight through it. The whole terrain check was rebuilt to be faster and to genuinely avoid unsuitable ground, and claiming now shows a "Claiming Plot..." message so it's clear something's happening.

New Commands

/rtp and /tpa added

/rtp randomly teleports you somewhere safe within the world border (with a cave option for underground). /tpa lets you send another player a teleport request they can accept or deny, same as most other servers. Neither works while you're tagged in combat.

Player Shops

Double chests now work properly as shops

Setting up a shop on a genuine double chest now correctly treats both halves as one combined 54-slot inventory, and breaking either half is protected the same as any single-chest shop.
