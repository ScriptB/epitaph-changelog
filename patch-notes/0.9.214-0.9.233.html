Epitaph · Patch Notes

Update 0.9.214–233

A plain-language look at this stretch of updates. For the full development log, see HISTORY.md.

Snapshot: ~July 27, 2026
Commands

⌨️ Every command now lives under one roof

All the separate command packs (/plot, /shop, /kit, and the rest) were consolidated into the main pack. Every command still works exactly the same — you'll just notice the colon-form version (like /plot:plot) showing up in autocomplete next to the normal one. Just ignore it and keep using the bare command as always.

Kits

🎁 Six new kits: Knight, Phantom, Brute, Sprinter, Gapple, and Titan

A batch of new kits was added, ranging from a solid unenchanted loadout all the way up to Titan — the single most valuable kit in the game, including totems, an elytra, and a maxed netherite pickaxe. /kit also gained real autocomplete for kit names, and /kit info <name> lets you check exactly what's in a kit before buying it.

Plots

🗺️ Choose your plot's biome when you claim

Claiming a plot now asks what biome you'd like — Plains, Desert, Ocean, and several others — and searches for a genuine match nearby before falling back to the normal random search if nothing suitable turns up close by. "No preference" still gives you the fastest possible claim.

Diagnostics

🧪 Plot claiming stopped getting permanently stuck

A rare bug could leave the plot-claiming system reporting the whole region was full even when it clearly wasn't. It now automatically recovers from this on its own, and /epitaph diag (admin-only) shows real live numbers if it's ever worth checking.

Bug Fixes

🐛 Smaller fixes worth knowing about

  • Picking up a wild mob spawner sometimes needed several tries — fixed.
  • Granting someone container access on your plot no longer also blocks them from mining or building.
  • /pay was rebuilt as a smaller, more reliable command.
