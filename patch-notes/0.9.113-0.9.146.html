Epitaph · Patch Notes

Update 0.9.113–146

The earliest stretch of updates this changelog series covers. A plain-language look at what changed for players across this range. For the full, warts-and-all development log, see HISTORY.md.

Plots & Permissions

🔐 Trust became real, per-permission access

Plot access used to be all-or-nothing: someone was either trusted or they weren't. It's now five separate toggles — build, break, containers, generators, and managing trust itself — so you can let a friend use your chests without also letting them mine your build, or the other way around.

A player-search screen was added too, so granting someone access doesn't require them to already be on a list first.

Generators & Spawners

⛏️ Generators no longer sit empty between cycles

A generator's output slot used to just sit as bare air between harvests. It now fills with a placeholder block and a bit of smoke so it reads as "working," not "broken." Mob generators also got a "Show Spawn Radius" button in their hub, so building a proper containment box around a spawner no longer means guessing.

PvP

⚔️ Kit Disparity and a real PvP Zone

The gear-gap protection that stops a heavily-kitted player from farming a fresh one got several real fixes — claiming kits out of order no longer lowers your protection, and it now also reflects your actual current gear, not just whatever kit you last claimed.

A new PvP Zone was added too: an admin-configured area where that gear-gap protection turns off entirely and kills pay bonus EXP and cash, for anyone who wants a genuine free-for-all spot.

Market & Shops

🛍️ Sell receipts, market rates, and a pre-sell preview

Selling now gives you a real itemized receipt instead of a flat "sold N stacks" message, and always points you toward Player Shops as the better long-term option. /market rates was added so you can check flat NPC prices without digging through the guide book, and /market sell now shows a preview of what's actually sellable before it places the chest.

The old "Bazaar" name for player-run shops was dropped in favor of just calling them Player Shops.

Plot Flight

🕊️ Plot Flight went through a few false starts

Flight on your own plot was rebuilt more than once trying to fix players getting stuck flying after leaving their plot. It landed on a fix that forces the game to properly sync your flight state the moment you leave, so you actually stop flying when you're supposed to.

Progression

📈 Level Shop, bigger Storage tiers, and Plot Crystal

A new Level Shop spends real vanilla XP (not your Epitaph EXP) on cosmetics and temporary potion-style boosts, unlocked once you hit level 30. Generator Storage got two new top tiers for players who wanted even bigger buffers. And Plot Crystal is a one-time purchase that gives every generator on your plot a flat speed boost at once, instead of upgrading each one individually.

Bug Fixes

🐛 A real batch of quality-of-life fixes

  • Generator leveling (the slow passive yield bonus every generator earns over time) was fully turned back on after being disabled.
  • Several menus, including Plot Permissions, stopped randomly closing on you mid-search.
  • Creative-mode players could no longer sell items or dodge the PvP gear-gap check by accident.
  • Netherite Block and several other block generators were producing far too slowly, and now run at their intended speed.
  • Picking up a wild mob spawner no longer crashes the menu.
  • A duplication exploit around illegally placed blocks was closed.

None of these added anything new, but each one was something players had actually run into.
