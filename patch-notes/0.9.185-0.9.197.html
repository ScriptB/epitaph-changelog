Epitaph · Patch Notes

Update 0.9.185–197

A plain-language look at this stretch of updates. For the full development log, see HISTORY.md.

Snapshot: ~July 25, 2026
Player Shops

Multi-item shops, and a real mailbox for overflow

A single chest can now sell several different items at once, each with its own price, instead of being locked to one item type. Items too big for your inventory when you buy something — including a fully loaded shulker box — now reliably land in your mailbox exactly as they were, instead of risking losing what was inside.

Sign colors were also tuned so the price and item name stay readable no matter which wood type your sign uses.

Raid Nights

Raid Nights redone around generators, not full plot access

Raid Night used to let anyone walk onto an online owner's plot and access almost anything. It's now scoped down: anyone can walk onto any plot during a raid, regardless of the owner's online status, but the only things fair game are mining a generator's own output and fighting mobs a spawner actually produced. Building, breaking, and containers stay fully protected the whole time. Every generator below the top tier also got a permanent speed boost.

New Feature

WAILA — see what you're looking at

A new look-at display shows details about whatever block or entity is in your crosshair: a generator's owner and level, a shop's price and seller, or a mob's name and health. It can be turned off in /hud config if you'd rather not have it on screen.

HUD

Every HUD element is now toggleable, and stays that way

Tips, personal stats, and leaderboards can all be turned on or off individually in /hud config, and your choices are personal and permanent — they won't reset or change on their own.

Server Stability

Player Shops stopped competing for a shared resource limit

Once enough Player Shops existed at once, the realm could start hitting a hard engine limit on active shop chests, which — worst case — could make plot claiming fail for someone else entirely unrelated. Shops now share a much larger pool that scales properly, so this can't happen regardless of how many shops are running.

Bug Fixes

/pay and /bounty actually work again

A full sanity sweep found and fixed the same name-resolution issue from earlier affecting /pay and /bounty — both had been silently failing for genuinely online, correctly-typed targets.
