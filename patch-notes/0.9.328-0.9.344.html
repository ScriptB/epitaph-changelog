Epitaph · Patch Notes

Update 0.9.328–344

A plain-language look at this stretch of updates. For the full development log, see HISTORY.md.

Snapshot: ~August 4, 2026
Plots

🏚️ Plot Member replaces Team

Team has been retired in favor of Plot Member, a permanent, admin-managed form of shared ownership. Inviting someone makes them a genuine co-owner of your plot with full access to everything on it, but at a real cost: their own plot is destroyed the moment they accept, so there's never a spare plot sitting around. Only an admin can remove a Member once they've joined.

Spawners

🦗 Multi-Spawn is now a real trade-off, not a free unlock

A spawner could previously mix in extra mob types for a flat EXP cost alone, letting a common spawner absorb a much rarer one's variety for cheap. Now, mixing in another type requires physically sacrificing a real spawner of that type by feeding it into the one you want to upgrade.

Anti-Dupe

🔒 More duplication tricks closed

Pushing a hopper and dropper together with a piston to bypass the earlier facing-loop protection no longer works. The game also now periodically checks for the classic "duplicate items via a disconnect" trick and alerts admins if it spots one.

Generators

🎯 Mob spawn containment is now much more reliable

A mob spawner's containment box could still let mobs slip past a wall in certain shapes, or spawn nowhere near a room's actual open floor. Both are fixed, and the in-game spawn radius preview now shows exactly where mobs can and can't land.
