Epitaph · Patch Notes

Update 0.9.147–166

A plain-language look at this stretch of updates. For the full development log, see HISTORY.md.

End Portals

🌀 Portal protection stopped fighting your own plot

Some players were being told an End Portal was protecting an area near their own claimed, authorized plot, even with no visible portal anywhere close. That protection now steps aside anywhere you're already allowed to build, and a new admin tool can check the nearest known portal's real distance if it ever looks wrong again.

World Border

🚧 The border warning stopped spamming half the map

The "approaching the world border" warning used to fire for anyone on a legitimately far-out, perfectly safe plot — over half the claimable area at the time. It's now scoped to only actually warn you when you're genuinely close to the edge.

Spawners

🧟 Spawners never worked, and the real cause was found

Every mob spawner in the game had been silently producing nothing, ever, since spawners were added — a placement math error meant the spawn search always died on its first check. That's fixed, and every spawner now has its own real, independent mob cap so a wild mob wandering nearby (or a neighboring spawner) can't throttle your own spawner's output.

Plots & Permissions

👥 A real player list, search, and Permission Groups

Plot Permissions got a proper overhaul: a browsable list of online players, real partial-name search instead of needing an exact spelling, and Permission Groups, so you can set up a "Builders" group once and just add or remove people from it instead of configuring each person by hand.

/plot info and /plot list both now mention which group someone belongs to, so it's clear where their access is actually coming from.

Ranks & Kits

🎖️ Ranks and rank-exclusive kits added

New Staff, Creator, and Beta Tester ranks were added, each with a small tag next to your name and a rank-exclusive kit. A genuine Realm Owner rank was also separated out from ordinary Operator status, since those aren't the same thing. Every admin-only screen in the game was re-audited to make sure nothing was reachable without the right permission.

Economy

💰 A proper Give menu, replacing "give myself cash"

The old give-cash tool only ever let an admin add to their own balance, with no way to fix an account that got stuck at some absurd number. The new Give menu works on any player, for Items, Cash, or EXP, and includes a real "Set Exact Value" and "Reset to 0" option.

Security Fixes

🛡️ A handful of real permission leaks closed

  • A player with genuine world Operator status (not necessarily a real admin) was silently bypassing build and break protection on every plot.
  • Disabling Entry for everyone on your plot wasn't actually kicking people who were already standing there.
  • Item frames, armor stands, and paintings had no plot protection at all, meaning anyone could break them regardless of permissions.
  • Placing an ordinary block like a crafting table in open wilderness was being denied and the item was lost outright, with no way to get it back — it's now allowed, and a denied placement gives your item back.
