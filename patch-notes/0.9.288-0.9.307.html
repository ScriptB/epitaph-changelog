Epitaph · Patch Notes

Update 0.9.288–307

A plain-language look at this stretch of updates. For the full development log, see HISTORY.md.

New Item

🪶 Introducing the Selly Stick

The Sell Stick from the last update went through a proper naming and polish pass and is now the Selly Stick — a real, glinting item with its own icon, rather than a reused vanilla one. It works exactly the same: sell a whole chest of ores at once.

Plots

🐾 Real protection for pets, animals, and villagers

There was previously nothing stopping a stranger from killing your animals or villagers on your own plot. A new permission specifically covers this, separate from the one that governs generators and spawners.

Survival

❤️ Healing now speeds up when you're well-fed

Previously, healing always ticked over at the same slow rate no matter how full your hunger bar was. Now, staying completely fed — full hunger and saturation — kicks you into a genuinely faster regeneration rate, the same way it works in the Java Edition of Minecraft. Let your hunger dip and healing slows back down.

Teleportation

🧭 Accepting a teleport request gives you a moment to settle in

Accept a friend's /tpa request and you'll no longer risk getting immediately shoved back off their plot if you don't already have permission to be there. For about two minutes after arriving, you can freely walk around and get your bearings — you still can't build, break, or touch anything you weren't already allowed to.

Plot Protection

🚧 Plot borders now feel like a real wall

Walking onto a plot you don't have permission for used to sometimes teleport you, occasionally a long way if your own plot was far off. It now works like the world border itself: you're physically stopped and pushed back right at the edge, exactly where you tried to cross.
