Generators & Economy

⚒️ Sacrifice a generator, boost your whole plot

If a generator's been maxed out for a while and you're not sure what else to do with it, you now have an option. Open its hub menu and sacrifice it. You get a cash refund back, plus a permanent speed bonus that applies to every generator on your plot, not just the one you gave up.

The better the generator, the bigger the bonus. Sacrificing a Diamond generator gives a noticeably bigger bump than sacrificing a Lapis one.

  • The refund is 40% of what you originally paid, not the full price.
  • The bonus caps out once your plot hits +50% total.
  • Spawners and Ancient Debris generators can't be sacrificed.
  • It's permanent. Any upgrades on the generator are gone too, no refund on those.

A real reason to keep interacting with generators once they're already maxed, instead of them just sitting there with nothing left to do.

Market

🛒 The Market now rotates in new stock

Open the Market and you'll see a new "Featured" tab. Every 12 hours it picks five generators from the regular catalog and puts them at 20% off for the rest of that window. There's also one exclusive item in there you can't buy anywhere else, rotating between two potions right now:

  • Market Rush — Haste II and Speed II for 15 minutes, $15,000
  • Adrenaline Rush — Strength II and Regeneration II for 10 minutes, $25,000

Buying from Featured works exactly like a normal Market purchase. If your inventory's full it gets mailed to you instead. The countdown on the screen tells you when the next restock happens.

Something worth checking back on, instead of the Market being a one-and-done list you never look at twice.

Plots & Teams

🏷️ Team names now show above your head

This one's a bug fix, but a lot of people asked about it. If your plot has a team name set, it used to only show up in menus and search screens. Now it also shows as a tag right above your head, the same way rank tags already do.

It updates right away too — set or rename your team, get invited, or get removed by an admin, and your tag changes on the spot instead of waiting around.

Your team was already real. Now it actually looks like one when you're standing next to your teammates.
