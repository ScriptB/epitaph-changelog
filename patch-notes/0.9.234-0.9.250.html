Epitaph · Patch Notes

Update 0.9.234–250

A plain-language look at this stretch of updates. For the full development log, see HISTORY.md.

Snapshot: ~July 28, 2026
Spawners

🕳️ Mob generators fixed around water and spawn spacing

Water sitting near a mob generator used to block off large parts of a room, forcing everything to spawn right on top of the generator itself. Mobs now spread out properly through water-adjacent rooms, the way a real farm expects them to.

Plots

🏛️ A floating name now marks every plot

Every claimed plot now shows a floating hologram of its owner's name, high above the build. It follows where you're actually building on larger plots instead of sitting fixed at dead center.

Economy

💵 Plot upgrades got dramatically cheaper

Flight, Border upgrades, and Plot Crystal were priced roughly 10,000 times higher than everything else in the game — a leftover from early testing that never got corrected. They're now priced in line with the rest of the economy: Flight is $500,000 instead of $5 billion, for example.

New Command

🕯️ /rtp cave added

/rtp now has a cave option that specifically looks for a genuine underground pocket instead of random surface terrain.

Bug Fixes

🐛 Deleting or resetting a plot now actually clears it

Delete Plot and Reset Plot used to leave every physical block behind despite saying otherwise. They now genuinely clear the build area first. A Storage upgrade purchased on a mob generator, which never did anything, is no longer sold at all.
