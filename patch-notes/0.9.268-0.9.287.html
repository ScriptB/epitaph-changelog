Epitaph · Patch Notes

Update 0.9.268–287

A plain-language look at this stretch of updates. For the full development log, see HISTORY.md.

Economy

🎯 A second way to earn EXP: kill mobs, not just players

EXP used to be earnable only by killing other players, which meant grinding upgrades often meant grinding your own friends. Killing ordinary mobs now earns EXP too, more if the mob came from your own spawner, and tough kills like a Ravager, Wither, or Ender Dragon pay out a genuinely large amount. Every generator upgrade cost was also rebalanced to match.

New Item

🪄 The Sell Stick — instantly sell a whole chest of ores

A new, very rare item can be found from tough boss kills. Shift-right-click or left-click it against a chest full of sellable ores and it sells everything at once, instantly, with a bonus the more you sell in one go. Only the plot owner or a permanent Member can use it, and only on their own plot.

Plots

🤝 Plot Teams added — real shared ownership

Adding a trusted player to your plot's Team gives them permanent access to your generators, spawners, chests, and shops, regardless of your other permission settings, and regardless of whether you're online. Only Team members can access your shop chests at all, even someone with full container permissions otherwise.

UI

🎨 Generator menus got a real readability pass

Several generator and spawner screens had button text that was hard to read against the menu background. They've all been recolored, and Prestige and mining a generator's block now show the item you're actually holding, not a placeholder ore block.

PvP

🚫 Repeated combat-logging now leads to a real ban

Disconnecting mid-fight already left a mark against your name. Now every strike also gives you a small permanent PvP damage penalty, and hitting five strikes results in a genuine, permanent removal from the realm.

Bug Fixes

🐛 Sweeping Edge and a couple of admin tools fixed

  • A clean sword swing's sweep attack was dealing zero damage to anyone but the main target — fixed.
  • An admin tool for editing an item you're holding (renaming it, adding Keep on Death) was added.
  • Some trespass-related teleports could land you somewhere unsafe — they now always land you somewhere confirmed safe.
